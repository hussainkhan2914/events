Project Overview

About CampusEvents

CampusEvents is a collegiate event management platform engineered to solve student awareness fragmentation, streamline online event registrations, and celebrate campus culture at Shadan College of Engineering and Technology.

Our Purpose

Campus events are traditionally promoted through scattered WhatsApp messages, hallway notice boards, and word-of-mouth. CampusEvents centralizes all academic, sporting, and cultural activities into a unified, reliable portal.

What Students Can Do

Students can filter competitions by domain, view dynamic category themes, sign up with their college roll number, generate printable digital entry passes, and track tournament standings on live leaderboards.

Event Categories

From the high-stakes Annual Cricket Tournament and Tech Hackathons to practical cloud computing workshops and inter-college quiz bowls, our 8 core categories cover every dimension of student development.

Why CampusEvents?

Unlike static demo templates, CampusEvents operates with client-side persistence, real-time search, category visual theme matching, responsive accessibility, and an administrator panel for event organizers.
